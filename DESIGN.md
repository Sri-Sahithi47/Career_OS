# CareerOS — Design A

The approved September 22 image prototype guides the sourcing workspace: a 216px charcoal sidebar, white canvas, dark ink, and restrained orange actions. Public Sans is self-hosted. Use 5px control corners, 1px neutral separators, readable tables, and consistent 8px spacing increments.

## Sourcing workflow

Results is the default tab. It aggregates current portal postings, with keyword, location, and engagement filters, eight-row pagination, a selected-job preview, original-posting links, and authenticated shortlist saving. Portals retains all source selection, rotation views, TEKsystems all-days runs, and AI cleanup controls. Activity exposes real run logs. The right inspector contains keywords, excluded titles, posting age, selected portals, and advanced browser settings. On narrow screens it becomes a disclosure above the results; tables scroll inside their container.

## Product truth

Use actual job data and counts. C2C mentions are screening evidence, not verified eligibility; restrictions take precedence. Never label every contract role C2C. No mockup sample jobs, schedules, or invented metrics enter the app. Saving uses the existing authenticated jobs and interest endpoints. AI cleanup remains explicitly user-triggered.
